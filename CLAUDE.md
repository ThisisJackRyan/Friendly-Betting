# Friendly Betting

## UI copy rules
- No eyebrow labels (small kicker text above headings).
- No decorative micro-taglines, bullet or icon-prefixed filler lines, or redundant nudge links. They overcrowd the UI.
- Every line of text must do a job: a heading, an instruction, a status, or an action. When in doubt, cut it.
- No em dashes. No made-up stats.
